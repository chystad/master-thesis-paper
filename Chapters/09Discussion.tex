For the Basilisk-Skyfield comparison, the TLE series propagation will also drift from drag caused by any and all attitude maneuvers. This can be seen in the difference between the first and last TLE mean motion (revolutions per day) estimate, which increase by 0.061 over the span of 130 days. The increase in daily revolutions correspond to a smaller semimajor axis, which is primarily caused by drag. A perfect match would therefore not necessarily mean perfect Basilisk prediction accuracy. Ideally, multiple TLE series should be compared and averaged to use as a baseline, but it is not feasible to find multiple TLE series that define the EXACT same orbit. Any deviation in orbital elements will manifest as a drift in the comparison. It might be possible to find 2 separate TLE series for 2 same-shaped spacecraft that have approximately the same position and orbit at different points in time, but this search and additional implementation complexity does not justify the presumably small increase "ground-truth" accuracy.
